# Privacy Policy — "AmSU Schedule"

[Русский](PRIVACY.md) · **English** · [中文](PRIVACY_ZH.md)

Effective date: October 26, 2026.

"AmSU Schedule" (`com.schedule.of.AmSU`) is an app for viewing the class schedule of Amur State University (AmSU). Developer: Alexey Evgenievich Ryzhkin. Contact: lexa0549@gmail.com.

## Data stored on your device

Settings, favorite groups and teachers, notes, appearance and the schedule cache are stored only on your device. A settings backup is created only at your request and saved to a file you choose.

## Data sent over the network

- **Schedule and university data.** The app contacts AmSU sites and servers (amursu.ru, cabinet.amursu.ru, newmoodle.amursu.ru) to get the schedule, teacher information and personal cabinet data. The login and password for the personal cabinet or Moodle are entered by you and are sent only to the university's servers.
- **Usage statistics.** At most once a day the app sends a random install ID, the app version and the date. If you are signed in to the personal cabinet, your study group number and student ID number are sent as well. The ID is not linked to your name or login. All of this can be turned off with the "Usage statistics" switch in the "About" section.
- **Crash reports.** After a crash, a technical report may be sent (app version, device model, error text). No personal data is included.
- **Update checks.** The app asks GitHub for information about a new version (not used in builds installed from app stores).

The app uses no advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Data is not sold or shared with third parties other than the services listed above that are needed for the app to work.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state** — downloading the schedule.
- **Location (approximate and precise)** — showing your position on the campus map. Location is processed on the device and is never transmitted.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, start after reboot** — class reminders.
- **Calendar (read and write)** — adding classes to your calendar at your request.
- **Biometrics** — confirming your identity when opening protected sections of the app.
- **Package installation** — installing an update that you confirm yourself.

Each permission is used only for the function described. You can revoke any permission in your device settings.

## Storage and deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To delete anonymous statistics records, write to lexa0549@gmail.com.

## Children

The app is intended for university students and teachers and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes

The current version of this policy is always available at this page's address. If the policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated.
